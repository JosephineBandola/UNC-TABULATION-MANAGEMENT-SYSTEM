Established to promote Bicol’s rich traditions, the competition highlights not only physical grace but also advocacy, leadership, and community involvement.

QUALIFICATIONS

🌟 BECOME THE NEXT MISS BICOLANDIA! 🌟

The countdown is on — the Miss Bicolandia season is just around the corner! Do you have the beauty, grace, and confidence to wear the crown? Check out the qualifications below and see if you’re ready to take the stage as the next Miss Bicolandia!
👑 MISS BICOLANDIA 2025: Qualifications
  • Must be between 17 and 28 years old by the coronation night
  • At least 5’3” tall without footwear
  • Single, with good moral standing and reputation, and has never been a mother or given birth
  • A Filipino citizen
  • A Bicolana by birth, blood, or residency (living in the Bicol Region for at least three consecutive years)
  • Must have completed at least Junior High School
  • Physically fit and healthy, capable of fulfilling her duties as Ambassadress of Goodwill for the Bicol Region
  • Must possess exceptional beauty, grace, and a pleasing personality
  • Must be available for all rehearsals, photoshoots, and official pageant events

REQUIREMENTS

👑 DOCUMENTS TO SUBMIT
  • Completed and signed application form
  • Valid proof of residency (e.g., Barangay certificate or utility bill)
  • Medical certificate stating you are fit to participate
  • Copy of the latest school ID or government-issued ID
